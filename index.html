What the data reveals

Data coverage

Not displayed does not mean zero. Some values are confidential or unavailable; this dashboard also uses a deliberately limited transcription of the official table. Do not interpret the visible bars as a complete national ranking.

How this was calculated

Shipment values are nominal Canadian dollars, not GDP, profits or physical production. Provincial non-coal totals come from the "Total annual mineral production, excluding coal" table. Gold shipment value and kilograms produced come from the "Metals → Gold" rows. Values stated in thousands of dollars were divided by 1,000,000 to display CA$ billions. Selected gold rows were transcribed; untranscribed cells are not evidence of confidentiality or zero. Refer to the full official table to distinguish confidential (x), unavailable (..) and untranscribed values. 2025 is preliminary; 2024 is revised. Source coverage and definitions may change with revisions.

Sources: Natural Resources Canada annual mineral tables · Statistics Canada Table 16-10-0022-02. Accessed October 29, 2026.
